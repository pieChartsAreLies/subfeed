# Couch test: 10 minutes with the remote

A manual check to run after installing the app, with the normal TV remote, once you have signed in with Google (or before, to see your seed channels). Tick each line. If something fails, note the video and what you pressed, and open an issue.

## 1. Open it (1 minute)

- [ ] Find **Subfeed** in the TV's app list. Open it.
- [ ] A screen of videos appears within a couple of seconds, even before it finishes refreshing.
- [ ] The big area at the top shows the focused video's picture, title, channel and how long ago it was posted.
- [ ] Along the top there are tabs (All, plus your categories) and Settings at the far right.

## 2. Move around (2 minutes)

- [ ] Press Right and Left a few times. Focus moves one video at a time.
- [ ] Press and hold Right. It jumps from channel to channel, and a small label shows the channel name.
- [ ] Press Up from the middle of the row. Focus goes to the tabs. Press Down. You land back on the same video.
- [ ] Press Up, then Right to another tab, then Down. Focus lands on the video you were halfway through, or else the newest unwatched one.
- [ ] Look through every tab. **No Shorts anywhere.** Nothing looks like a recommendation.

## 3. Play something (3 minutes)

- [ ] Press OK on a video. It starts playing within a few seconds. **Nothing is drawn over the picture.**
- [ ] Press Right. A bar with the title, position and chapter marks appears, then fades after about 3 seconds on its own.
- [ ] Press Right a few times to jump ahead, Left to go back. Skipping works and picture and sound stay together.
- [ ] If the video has chapters: press Up to show the bar, Up again to open the action row, then Right to **Next chapter** and OK. The CH+ / CH- buttons on the remote also jump between chapters.
- [ ] Pause with Play/Pause. Wait a moment. Press it again to resume.
- [ ] Press Back once (the bar hides), then Back again (you leave). You are back on the same video in the row, and it shows a progress bar.

## 4. Sponsor skip (1 minute)

- [ ] Play a video from a channel that runs sponsor reads (most tech channels do).
- [ ] When a sponsor segment starts, the video jumps past it and a note says "Skipped sponsor · press Down to undo" for 3 seconds.
- [ ] Press Down while the note shows. It goes back to just before the segment and does not skip it again for that video.

## 5. Up next (1 minute)

- [ ] Let a short video finish. A full-screen "Up next" card appears and counts down 5 seconds, then plays the next unwatched video in the row. Press Back to stop instead.
- [ ] While a video plays, **nothing about the next video appears over it.** The card only shows after the video ends.

## 6. Menu and Settings (2 minutes)

- [ ] Press and hold OK on a video. A menu opens: Save to the NAS, Play from start, Go to channel, Mark watched or unwatched, Move channel, Hide from Subfeed.
- [ ] Mark one video watched. It dims with a check mark. Mark it unwatched again.
- [ ] Open Settings (far right tab, or the Menu button). It shows plain-sentence health lines with green or red dots, and **Sign in with Google**.
- [ ] If you have not signed in yet: it shows a code to type at google.com/device.

## 7. Captions and pass or fail

- Pass: every box ticked, with no stalls longer than a couple of seconds and no Shorts.
- Captions: play a video, press Up, Up, choose **Captions: Off** and press OK. Captions appear (English, or the video's own language). Do the same to turn them off.
- Not part of this test: saving a video and watching away from home.

If the screensaver matters to you, leave a video paused for about 5 minutes and see whether your TV's screensaver starts.
